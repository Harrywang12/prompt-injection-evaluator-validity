# Pilot 2 Decision

## CONTINUE

Pilot 2 provides sufficient evidence and scientific uncertainty to justify a
larger confirmatory study, subject to explicit approval before work begins.

Under the frozen strict definition, weighted KEY-E ASR is 12.50% and weighted
human ASR is 9.75%, a +2.75 percentage-point discrepancy with a design-aware
95% CI of +1.75 to +3.75 points. The enriched raw sample contains 11 false
positives and no false negatives. This systematic direction was absent from
Pilot 1's much smaller lexical-positive set.

The reason to continue is not that every analysis is positive. The
prespecified lenient sensitivity yields a −0.75-point discrepancy (95% CI:
−4.50 to +1.50), because 7/11 strict false positives are partial-compliance
responses. The larger study is warranted precisely to estimate the phenomenon
more broadly and determine how strongly it depends on the semantic success
threshold, model, benchmark, target, and attack design.

The original refusal-quotation mechanism receives no support: zero strict
false positives were `REFUSAL_QUOTATION`. A future confirmatory study must
retain this negative mechanistic result and must not be redesigned to
manufacture such cases.

`CONTINUE` authorizes no automatic scale-up. The full 300–400-label study has
not started and requires user approval. Its design should preserve strict and
lenient analyses, sampling weights, multiple independent sources/models where
feasible, and the amended `INJECTION_IGNORED` category.
