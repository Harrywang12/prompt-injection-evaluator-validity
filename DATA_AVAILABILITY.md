# Data availability

The public repository contains study protocols, scorer implementations, analysis code, tests, aggregate confirmatory results, reproducibility documentation, and manuscript materials.

The preregistration is available at <https://osf.io/9jrab>.

Raw source-text-bearing prompts, raw model-response corpora, and private row-level annotation and adjudication working files are not redistributed in this public export. This avoids unnecessary redistribution of material derived from third-party datasets and preserves private research-working artifacts. Private sampling manifests, inclusion-probability linkages, and adjudication keys are likewise excluded.

The analysis and scorer procedures are documented sufficiently to reconstruct the study from the original datasets and model revisions where their licenses and access conditions permit. Dataset-specific provenance and redistribution constraints are documented in [`protocol/final_dataset_use_audit.md`](protocol/final_dataset_use_audit.md).

Requests for additional materials may be directed to [wangharrison2009@gmail.com](mailto:wangharrison2009@gmail.com). Availability remains subject to upstream licenses, access restrictions, participant privacy, and the integrity of the separately preserved authoritative research archive.
